Capabilities

What CIG Does

HPC
High-Performance Computing

OMEGA's architecture leverages heterogeneous node arrays — GPU, TPU, and custom ASIC — interconnected by our proprietary low-latency mesh fabric, delivering sustained petaflop throughput for real-world workloads.

ML
Machine Learning Research

Our ML Division publishes open research on scalable training infrastructure, novel optimizer architectures, and emergent reasoning benchmarks. Collaboration with universities across 14 countries.

SIM
Simulation & Modeling

From protein folding at atomic resolution to city-scale traffic optimization, OMEGA's simulation layer handles stochastic and deterministic workloads with reproducible precision.

SYS
Distributed Systems Engineering

CIG engineers design fault-tolerant, globally distributed pipelines that maintain consistency under adversarial network conditions — a core competency of the OMEGA platform.

SEC
Secure Compute Environments

All OMEGA workloads run within cryptographically isolated enclaves. Zero-trust networking, hardware attestation, and continuous audit trails ensure data sovereignty for all research partners.

TAL
Talent & Community

Through our Coding Challenges and open research programs, CIG cultivates a global community of engineers, researchers, and problem-solvers — the human layer behind OMEGA's breakthroughs.
