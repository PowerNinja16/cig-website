About Us

Built by Researchers.
Driven by Curiosity.

Collective Intelligence Group was founded in 2017 by a coalition of computational scientists, systems engineers, and AI researchers who believed that the next era of discovery would require infrastructure that didn't yet exist. Seven years later, we've built it — and we're still building.

Our Story

From a Blueprint
to a Breakthrough

CIG began as a research collective operating out of three universities simultaneously. Our founding team shared a dissatisfaction with the compute bottlenecks that were throttling scientific progress — not just in AI, but across genomics, climate science, and materials research. The vision was clear: build the system the research community actually needed.

OMEGA was initially conceived as a heterogeneous cluster experiment. Within two years, it had outpaced every academic supercomputer it was benchmarked against. That success attracted talent, funding, and an ever-growing roster of research partners. Today, OMEGA sits at the frontier of what is computationally possible.

We remain independent, research-first, and deeply committed to operating transparently at the edge of the field.

You have 72 hours. Any language. Any approach. Solutions are scored on correctness, runtime efficiency, and — for top scorers — code elegance as reviewed by OMEGA engineers.

Submissions run through OMEGA's automated evaluation harness. Leaderboards update in real time. Top performers are notified directly — and the winner is contacted within 48 hours.
